## Credibility / Operating Model

### Research in the product. Systems in the process.

Use research where it prevents expensive mistakes. Build systems where they compound speed. Make every design decision traceable.

**Operating model tags**
Research-First · Systems Thinking · Dev-Ready Handoff · Ownership · Judgment Over Output

---

**How we work**

**Research-Grounded When It Matters**
Qualitative insight, usability testing, and behavioural analysis focused on decisions with the highest downstream cost.

**Systems-First by Default**
Every engagement leaves behind a stronger foundation — components, patterns, and documentation that outlast the project.

---

### Operating Principle 01 — Design-first, not decoration-last
Design enters the product conversation at strategy level, not after engineering has already committed.

### Operating Principle 02 — Opinionated but collaborative
We bring strong points of view, change them when the data says to, and document the reasoning either way.

### Operating Principle 03 — Built for messy realities
Shifting requirements, legacy constraints, under-resourced teams — the work adapts without losing design integrity.

**01 — Enterprise SaaS / Redesign**

### Procurement Platform Redesign

A full UX overhaul for a procurement tool used across 14 enterprise accounts, collapsing a fragmented workflow into a single coherent surface.

**Delivered**

Designed the full product experience for a savings app targeting first-time investors — from concept sprint to final dev handoff in 11 weeks.

**Delivered**
- research synthesis from 18 user interviews
- end-to-end iOS app design across 60+ screens
- motion and interaction spec for engineering

**Outcome**

Built the foundational design system for a B2B platform scaling from 3 to 24 engineers, with zero existing design documentation.

**Delivered**
- 120+ component Figma library with variant documentation
- token system covering typography, colour, spacing, and elevation
- contribution guidelines and governance model

**Outcome**
Cut design-to-dev cycle time by half within two product quarters. Onboarded three new designers in days, not weeks.

## About

### Built to think, design, and ship with ambitious teams.

> Great products don't come from more features. They come from sharper decisions made earlier.

Folio started as a small UX consultancy solving onboarding problems for a single SaaS client. The work expanded into product thinking, system building, and embedded design leadership.

Today the right fit is a team that needs more than visual output. They need a design partner who can question the brief, map the system, and stay in the room until it's shipped.

Considered in approach. Decisive in execution. Protective of quality throughout.
